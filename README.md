# Rollcall Event

**Rollcall Event** is a prototype event management system focused on real-time participant check-in, oversight, and safe execution of corporate trips and events.

The core idea is simple:  
**know who is present, who is missing, and what is happening – at all times.**

---

## Concept

Managing participant flow during corporate trips involves more than just registration lists. Organizers need live insight into arrivals, movements between locations, parallel activities, and deviations from the plan.

Rollcall Event is designed to support this by combining:
- fast and flexible check-in
- real-time status tracking
- clear operational dashboards
- simple incident and deviation handling

The system aims to reduce manual coordination, improve safety, and give organizers confidence throughout the event lifecycle.

---

## Key Capabilities

### Check-In & Status
- Multiple check-in methods (digital and manual)
- Real-time participant presence tracking
- Clear indication of missing or unregistered participants

### Real-Time Oversight
- Live overview of participants across locations and activities
- Support for parallel sessions, transport legs, and split itineraries
- Operational dashboards for organizers

### Participant Information
- Quick access to essential details such as:
  - contact information
  - special needs or allergies
  - room or group assignments

### Notifications & Updates
- Simple messages to participants about:
  - schedule changes
  - meeting points
  - practical updates
- Shared, up-to-date event schedule

### Safety & Incident Handling
- Clear lists of missing participants
- Support for documenting incidents and deviations
- Structured follow-up after the event

---

## Scope and Goals

This project is developed as a **student prototype**, with focus on:
- clear domain modeling
- realistic event scenarios
- practical usability for organizers
- clean and extensible architecture

The solution is **not intended as a full production system**, but is designed so that core concepts could be extended into a future product or spin-off.

---

## Target Use Cases

- Corporate trips and retreats
- Conferences and multi-day events
- Group travel with structured programs
- Events where participant safety and oversight are critical
